Movement calibration

The game learns how your face moves for each emotion. Then, at every moment, it asks: which of your own emotions does your face look like right now? All data on this page is real, from your session on 5 October (dd4b60) and from 9 sessions in total.

How it works, in 3 steps

2 · every secondCompare Your face now is compared with the 7 remembered faces. The model's own opinion is added.
3 · every secondDecide The emotion with the best match wins.

Part 2 · The details
Before everything

What is one "moment"? Frames, windows and ticks

frame · 10 per secondOne picture of the face The headset measures 70 face values (63 face muscles + 7 tongue values), 10 times per second.
window · 5 frames = 0.5 sA short clip The last 5 frames together. A short clip shows the movement, not only one picture.
tick · once per secondOne answer Once per second the game takes the newest window and gives one answer. On this page, a "moment" is one tick.

How to read: the top line is one trial: you hold one emotion for about 10 seconds, and the game answers once per second (ticks 0 to 9). Tick 0 is skipped, because you have only just started. The bottom line zooms into one second: about 10 frames arrive, and only the last 5 form the window for that tick.

How to read: this is a real window from your session (an Anger moment). Each row is one frame, oldest at the top. Each column is a muscle. The model reads all 5 rows (5 × 70 numbers). The face match uses the bottom row: the mean of the 5 frames for the 63 face muscles.

How to read: the top row is your face at this moment. The rows below are the two closest typical faces. If the dark cells sit in the same columns, the patterns match. All rows are scaled to the same strength, because only the pattern counts.

b · The score of each emotion

face match model's opinion match total
  1. Face match: how alike the pattern of your face is to that emotion's typical face (1 = the same pattern, 0 = nothing in common).
  2. Model's opinion match: the same comparison for the model's 7 scores. These are close to 1 for every emotion, so only small differences matter.
  3. Total = face match + model's opinion match. The highest total wins.

Limits

8 of the 9 sessions are the same person (the one other person also improves, 0.58 → 0.65). Calibration and test were in the same session, minutes apart. Each emotion was calibrated only once. Fear is still hard: it is often taken for Surprise. Neutral gets worse than with no calibration (0.94 → 0.78).
